About the company

Service built around your needs

V. UMOREN MULTI SERVICES LTD is a Nigerian multi-service company focused on supplying products and delivering practical technical and general services to individual, commercial and institutional clients.

We aim to make it easier for customers to request services, source equipment and materials, and connect with dependable service support through one point of contact.

Our service commitment

We value professionalism, clear communication, responsible procurement, quality workmanship and customer satisfaction.

Vision

To build a trusted and accessible multi-service company delivering useful solutions and value to customers and communities.

Mission

To provide responsive services and dependable supplies through practical expertise, integrity and continuous improvement.
